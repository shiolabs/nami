On a Mac, apps from Homebrew's casks show their real icons, the nami app updates nami with a button, and nami updates itself once a day.

## Macs (preview)
- Mac apps show their own icons in the nami app: in Discover, in search, on their pages and in Installed. That's nearly every app nami can install from Homebrew's casks. The most popular ones come with nami's catalog. The rest come the first time an app is shown, each checked against the catalog's signature.
- Apps sold on the Mac App Store show its screenshots on their pages.
- An app without an icon shows its name's first letter, not a "C".
- nami updates itself once a day, along with the catalog and the apps installed for everyone, as it does on Linux. updates: manual in /etc/nami/system.nami turns that off. A Mac that was asleep updates when it wakes.
- In the nami app, Update in Installed updates nami. macOS asks for an administrator's password, then nami updates. Before, you copied a command into Terminal.
- The nami app's main buttons, like Install, are in your Mac's accent colour, as in other Mac apps. It's blue unless you picked another in System Settings.
- Apps whose download is a disk image inside a zip install, as with Homebrew.
