nami is no longer a preview on a Mac, and its daily update keeps your Mac apps up to date.

## Macs
- nami on a Mac is no longer a preview. What's different on a Mac is listed in [Install on a Mac](https://namios.dev/docs/nami/install#install-on-a-mac).
- The daily update now updates Mac apps from Homebrew's casks too: the apps you installed for everyone, and each person's own apps. Apps that update themselves, like Google Chrome, are left to do so.
- An app that's open when the daily update runs stays as it is, so nothing changes under you. It's updated the next time, once you've quit it.
- The notification after the daily update names the apps that were updated.
- Each person's older changes are now cleaned up daily on a Mac too, as their own file says.

## Fixes
- On a Mac, when nami apply leaves out what's only for Linux, it names what it leaves out without a doubled colon: "leaving out services and namios: Linux's, not on a Mac".
