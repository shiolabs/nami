Fixes for Macs: Mac apps install from the nami app on a new Mac, VLC and apps like it open, and the installer works on an account that isn't an administrator.

## Macs (preview)
- On a Mac where nothing was installed from Terminal yet, every app you installed from the nami app failed with "No such file or directory". The nami app now makes your user.nami first, as nami install does.
- Some apps, like VLC, keep part of their signature beside their files. nami's copy left it out, so macOS said it couldn't check the developer and offered to move the app to the Trash. nami keeps it now. Reinstall an app that macOS stopped this way.
- If an app's developer didn't sign it the way macOS wants, nami says so when it installs the app, and how to open it anyway.
- When an app couldn't install, its Details link stays in view on its card.
- On an account that isn't an administrator, the installer asks for an administrator's name and password in macOS's own box. Before, it stopped because sudo didn't work.
- The installer says that nami needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer before it downloads anything. On an older Mac, the programs nami installs don't start.
- The nami app in Applications belongs to the system, not to the account that installed it.
- Without sudo, nami self-uninstall also points to Uninstall nami… in the nami app, which asks for an administrator.
