# Changelog

What's new in each release of nami, newest first. Each version links to its
full notes, the ones the nami app shows in Updates.

## [0.22.4](docs/releases/0.22.4.md) · 2026-11-28

nami is no longer a preview on a Mac, and its daily update keeps your Mac apps up to date.

## [0.22.3](docs/releases/0.22.3.md) · 2026-11-28

The nami app shows how much space nami takes, and frees what it doesn't need with Clean Up.

## [0.22.2](docs/releases/0.22.2.md) · 2026-11-27

On a Mac, apps from Homebrew's casks show their real icons, the nami app updates nami with a button, and nami updates itself once a day.

## [0.22.1](docs/releases/0.22.1.md) · 2026-11-27

Fixes for Macs: Mac apps install from the nami app on a new Mac, VLC and apps like it open, and the installer works on an account that isn't an administrator.

## [0.22.0](docs/releases/0.22.0.md) · 2026-11-27

On a Mac, nami installs Mac apps from Homebrew's casks and puts your apps in Applications, the nami app comes to ARM Linux, and nami is easy to uninstall.

## [0.21.0](docs/releases/0.21.0.md) · 2026-11-27

nami runs on Macs as a preview, and on 64-bit ARM computers with Linux.

## [0.20.1](docs/releases/0.20.1.md) · 2026-11-26

Run any command without installing it with nami try, and namiOS's window is nami try-os.

## [0.20.0](docs/releases/0.20.0.md) · 2026-11-25

nami is open source, its own files move to /nami, and you can install nami without its app, or remove the app for good.

## [0.19.0](docs/releases/0.19.0.md) · 2026-11-25

nami now writes the recipe for a project from its link and builds it, and the install script works again on every Linux distribution.

Earlier versions were alpha builds from before nami's code was public.
